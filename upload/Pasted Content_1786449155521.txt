### Photo Placeholder System
EVERY image slot follows this exact pattern:
```html
<div class="photo-frame">
  <img id="photo-[unique-id]" 
       src="" 
       alt="[descriptive alt text]"
       class="photo-img"
       onerror="this.parentElement.classList.add('no-image')">
  <div class="photo-fallback">
    <span class="photo-initial">[INITIALS or LABEL]</span>
  </div>
</div>
```
CSS: .photo-frame has position:relative overflow:hidden
     .photo-img covers 100% with object-fit:cover
     .photo-fallback is absolute-positioned gradient behind the img
     When src="" or broken, gradient fallback is visible

Unique gradient per area:
  Workspace photos:   linear-gradient(135deg, #0D1B2A, #1a3a5c)
  Team portraits:     linear-gradient(135deg, #0D1B2A, #BC1A2C 120%)
  Cafeteria:          linear-gradient(135deg, #1a2010, #3a4a25)
  Advisory:           linear-gradient(135deg, #0D1B2A, #2d3a4f)
  Event/inauguration: linear-gradient(135deg, #1a0f0f, #3a1515)

### The Signature Element — Torii Gate

  - Two vertical posts + two horizontal bars (top bar curved upward at ends)
  - Always STROKE only, never filled
  - Color: var(--crimson) or white depending on context
  - Used 3 times maximum on the page:
    1. As navbar logo icon (28px, crimson)

### Motion Principles

               transform: translateY(32px) opacity:0 → translateY(0) opacity:1
               transition: 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
               Stagger siblings: delay +0.1s each

Hover on cards: translateY(-8px) + shadow deepen, 0.35s ease
Image zoom:     scale(1.04) on hover, overflow:hidden on container
Counter anim:   count up from 0 to value on first scroll into view

@media(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) → disable ALL
