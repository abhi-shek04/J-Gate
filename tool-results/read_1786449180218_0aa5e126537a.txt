   332→### Photo Placeholder System
   333→EVERY image slot follows this exact pattern:
   334→```html
   335→<div class="photo-frame">
   336→  <img id="photo-[unique-id]" 
   337→       src="" 
   338→       alt="[descriptive alt text]"
   339→       class="photo-img"
   340→       onerror="this.parentElement.classList.add('no-image')">
   341→  <div class="photo-fallback">
   342→    <span class="photo-initial">[INITIALS or LABEL]</span>
   343→  </div>
   344→</div>
   345→```
   346→CSS: .photo-frame has position:relative overflow:hidden
   347→     .photo-img covers 100% with object-fit:cover
