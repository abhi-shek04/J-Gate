## ❯ WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT FROM EVERY OTHER COWORKING SITE

After researching 200+ coworking websites — WeWork, Industrious, Second Home, 
Workco, Kilo Collective, 59 Rose Street — the pattern is clear:
The sites that WIN do 3 things the others don't:
  1. REAL photos lead everything — not copy, not icons, not illustrations
  2. The TEAM has faces, names, and stories — not "our passionate professionals"
  3. The SPACE is shown room-by-room, not just one hero shot

J-Gate is not a generic coworking space. It is a sovereign gateway between 
Japan and India. The design must feel like Industrious met Japan House met a 
luxury hotel lobby. Boutique hotel service standards applied to a business hub.

Photo upload system: The page must include clearly labeled `<img>` tags with 
`id` attributes for every photo slot — team photos, office photos, canteen 
photos — so the client can swap in real images instantly by replacing the `src`.
Every `<img>` must have a CSS gradient fallback that looks premium even before 
real photos are uploaded.

### Photo Placeholder System (CRITICAL)
Every image slot must follow this pattern:
```html
<img 
  id="photo-team-tanji"           <!-- unique ID for easy replacement -->
  src=""                          <!-- empty src = CSS fallback shows -->
  alt="Mr. Daisuke Tanji, Founder of J-Gate"
  class="jg-photo"
  onerror="this.style.display='none'"
>
<div class="jg-photo-placeholder" aria-hidden="true">
  <!-- Gradient background + initials shown when no photo loaded -->
</div>
```
CSS for placeholder:
```css
.jg-photo-placeholder {
  background: linear-gradient(135deg, #0D1B2A 0%, #1A2E45 50%, #BC1A2C 100%);
  display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center;
  font-family: 'Noto Serif JP'; color: rgba(255,255,255,0.6); font-size: 1.5rem;
}
```

- Image hover: scale(1.04) with overflow:hidden on container
- Photo gallery: lightbox on click (pure CSS/JS, no library)
- @media(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): disable ALL animation
